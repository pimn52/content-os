# Content OS — Durable Decisions

This file records choices that should remain stable across multiple work packages. It is not a product specification or experiment history. Current product behavior lives in docs/product/.

## D001 — Workspace/IP is the durable product center

Content OS is organized around a persistent creator/brand workspace and reusable context/assets, not around isolated generated videos.

## D002 — Local-first Data + Hybrid Compute

Creator assets, identity/profile, project state, evidence and control remain local-first. Heavy inference may run locally or through an explicitly approved remote/BYOK provider when quality, privacy, hardware, latency, license or cost requires it. Local-first does not mean local-inference-only.

## D003 — Provider-neutral product semantics

Core product contracts describe intent and evidence, not vendor/model implementation details. Provider-specific parameters remain in adapters/settings schemas.

## D004 — Narrative intent stays independent of provider limits

ScenePlan says what the content needs editorially. Execution Planner adapts that intent to current provider/runtime/machine capability. A temporary model limit must not become a permanent narrative rule.

## D005 — Real media first

Prefer adequate creator-owned/historical media before expensive generation. Missing media may produce an optional capture task before AI generation.

## D006 — MasterNarration is the authoritative narrated audio timeline

The product should not require manual per-scene narration cutting. Provider calls may operate on bounded takes/slices, but final narrated assembly maps back to one verified MasterNarration.

## D007 — TalkingRun is the product-level Talking result

Short provider slices/child jobs are execution details. A continuous creator-visible result becomes a product capability only when it is represented as a reviewed TalkingRun and admitted as a first-class generated Asset/Clip. For a composed TalkingRun, MasterNarration is the authoritative final audio.

## D008 — Multi-slice Talking requires source-forward continuous reference

Before dispatching a multi-short Talking series, Content OS maps all slices onto one authorized continuous source-performance run. Children may not independently restart at source time zero, loop unrelated footage or silently choose unrelated reference material.

## D009 — Terminal face closeout is product intent, not a vendor knob

The product may request a face-visible natural mouth close when final speech ends. An adapter may implement it through provider-native controls or validated Content OS context-and-crop logic. Intermediate TalkingRun slices are continuation slices; only the final delivered slice may apply terminal closeout.

## D010 — Capability evidence is configuration-scoped

Verified capability belongs to provider + model/version + runtime + machine/profile. One machine's result does not silently become another machine's verified default. Unknown remains unknown.

## D011 — One configuration precedence

Effective provider parameters resolve as:

    per-job override
    > saved provider+machine override
    > locally verified value
    > provider conservative default
    > unknown

Advanced Settings is an override surface over this same system, not a parallel configuration stack.

## D012 — Human quality gates remain explicit

Automated QA may prove copy/timing/playability/integrity. It does not prove creator likeness, naturalness, visible lip-sync, continuity or publishability.

## D013 — R1 remains a modular monolith

Keep FastAPI + SQLite + local Job/Worker + provider adapters + Remotion/FFmpeg. Growing orchestration should move into application services before considering distributed infrastructure.

## D014 — Completed capability and archived document are different concepts

When work completes, current product behavior belongs in the relevant product/module spec, implementation details remain in Git/evaluation evidence, and STATUS removes the completed package. docs/history/ is used only for an entire superseded document with historical reading value.

## D015 — Narration performance intent is editorial semantics, not a Voice knob

Delivery direction (emphasis, pace, pauses and rhetorical rhythm) belongs to an editable, exact-copy-bound Narration Performance Plan. It records source and evidence references, versions with the Draft, and is cleared on a copy edit rather than silently retargeted.

Provider adapters may apply an immutable snapshot only through an explicit capability and application receipt. That receipt, automated Voice QA and U-Voice are separate evidence; none may be substituted for another.

Content OS may compile the plan into exact-copy delivery segments before execution. The derived structure creates no timing or audio; it remains separate from any adapter application receipt.

## D016 — U-Voice is an immutable asset-level gate

After automated Voice QA, a U-Voice record must retain concrete findings and explicit judgments for likeness, naturalness, emphasis, pace, pauses and rhetorical rhythm. Only an all-pass record approves the exact generated Voice asset for Talking or final assembly.

A rejected asset cannot be silently re-approved in place. Human feedback is
quality/provenance evidence; it does not verify provider capabilities or
promote a delivery preference to a default.

## D017 — Rhetorical delivery assistance remains transparent editorial input

The product may derive reviewable delivery suggestions from general copy structure such as sentence role, parallel claims, assertion boundaries and enumerations. Each suggestion stays exact-copy-bound and records its structural rationale.

Suggestions are ephemeral assisted input: they are neither automatically saved nor executed, do not invoke a Voice provider, and do not claim acoustic quality or provider application. An individual U-Voice judgment cannot silently become a global learned default; a creator must explicitly edit/save the normal Narration Performance Plan.

## D018 — Rhetorical guidance is evidence-bounded semantic review, not an acoustic formula

Prosody research and public-speaking practice may inform the editorial language of Content OS: concept landing in parallel/contrastive claims, a reviewable claim or turn boundary, and locally driven enumeration within an otherwise conversational delivery.

They do not license a universal WPM, pause duration, F0, intensity, word-stress or provider setting. Overall pace, local pace and semantic pause boundaries remain distinct plan fields. A suggestion is still separate from adapter application receipt, Voice QA and U-Voice evidence; language, creator, genre, provider and human review determine any eventual realization.

## D019 — Partial Voice-plan coverage fails closed before execution

A provider may distinguish full, partial and unsupported coverage of a
Narration Performance Plan. Partial coverage is a preflight fact, not an
application receipt and not permission to silently omit cues.

Until the product exposes an explicit, creator-reviewed subset mode, a partial
or unsupported result must reject before ProviderCall reservation and before
the provider receives copy or reference media. A full application receipt,
automated Voice QA and U-Voice remain separate downstream evidence.

## D020 — Provider-neutral does not mean provider-shopping

Provider abstraction is a long-term replacement and routing boundary. It is not a requirement to integrate or benchmark many providers before one route can ship.

Reuse an admitted route while it meets the current plan's quality/capability needs. A concrete quality, capability, privacy or license blocker can justify a separately scoped alternative; do not benchmark providers merely to widen the catalog. Current provider focus belongs in the module spec, not a permanent vendor lock-in decision.

This decision does not convert benchmark-only model weights into a commercial-safe dependency; license admission remains separate from product-quality admission.

## D021 — R1 Voice judges publishable delivery, not perfect rhetorical control

Fine-grained word focus and whole-sentence intonation remain valuable Voice
improvements, but verified automatic control of either is not a hard admission
prerequisite for this product version. Do not hold an otherwise clear,
creator-like, natural and publishable short-form narration until an adapter can
prove full semantic Performance Plan execution.

This does not waive the asset-specific U-Voice gate. Reviewers still judge
emphasis and rhetorical rhythm, and reject an asset when flat or misplaced
delivery materially harms understanding or publishability. A dimension can
pass when delivery is adequate for publication; it need not be ideal or prove
generalized word-level control. Record imperfections and keep improving them
without turning one bounded human acceptance into a provider capability claim.

## D022 — Visual direction is a product layer, not renderer tuning

ScenePlan owns editorial meaning, Hybrid Asset Router owns the material route, and VideoSpec owns deterministic rendering.

The presentation decision belongs to a provider/render-neutral EditPlan: framing, text/subtitles, graphics, transitions, style and fallback. Initial visual intent, suitability and execution/cost feasibility precede generation; admitted results and measured timing then resolve the plan into VideoSpec. Do not generate first and discover the visual concept afterward.

Unknown crop safety must not become a fixed crop. Containment is a conservative geometry, not a publishability guarantee; use another eligible interval/treatment or stop if quality is inadequate. Eligibility binds to source, used interval and transform, not conversational duration examples.

A successful assisted render does not prove product-generated planning. R1 still requires the normal product flow to produce the scene/edit plan rather than relying on an implementation script.

## D023 — Automation progress is less manual work at preserved quality

Track contract implementation, runtime evidence, normal-flow integration, repeatability and automatic decisions separately. Package completion does not imply all five. Retain exact asset approvals without repeating answered reviews; consolidate subjective gates only through an explicit policy migration, preserving technical QA and exception handling.

Human feedback becomes reusable capability only when a scoped, versioned and reversible rule/preference actually changes a subsequent decision with regression evidence. Record manual actions/time, failures and total production effort alongside quality; a cheap failed render is not progress.

## D024 — Assisted source review is reusable evidence, not executable authority

A `review_claimed_suitable` Talking source assessment is an exact, versioned claim about one source-native portrait Asset/Clip interval. It does not by itself authorize a Talking Job. A complete assisted full-interval review may be reused across planned runs without repeated human review only when its reviewer/operator authority and evidence artifact are established by a trusted product action, not by caller-supplied `reviewer_reference` or `evidence_reference` strings. Fixture, partial, unknown, horizontal-crop and unauthenticated claims never become positive dispatch evidence. A failed claim is a visible replan signal, not proof of automatic detection.

Before planned dispatch, an admission receipt must bind the trusted actor/action, review method/version and inspectable evidence identity to the exact source bytes, Clip interval and presentation, and to the plan's required source window/quality constraints. It must be independently verifiable by the application. A trusted reviewer may adopt previously retained evidence after inspecting it, instead of repeating an already answered subjective review. A shared optional API token does not identify a reviewer. Where that trust boundary is absent, fail closed even if all four review dimensions say `pass`; do not add a one-off approval flag to the ProductionRun.

Each dispatch must revalidate the fresh plan and Master interval, source and evidence identity, current rights/consent (not merely nonempty references), exact provider/model/runtime capability and applicable use license, and current budget. Commercial-safe admission remains the implemented default; D029 defines a future explicit internal-evaluation scope. The Worker must recheck execution-sensitive gates before reserving the durable ProviderCall; a prior candidate binding is not a lasting authorization. A stale or revoked condition returns a named stop/replan state without paid inference. Generated Talking QA, human review and Run admission remain separate downstream gates. The existing direct Talking Job/series APIs are not mediated by this ProductionRun source-review gate and must be isolated or brought under equivalent admission before a system-wide dispatch claim.

This decision allows a narrow future trusted-attestation lane, not an automatic visual-suitability claim or a general horizontal-to-portrait derivation policy. The latter needs its own interval/crop/subtitle/quality evidence.

## D025 — A single planned child enters the existing Run contract through explicit origin evidence

A TalkingRun may cover one generated child. The existing series contract already permits a one-element ordered collection; the missing capability is explicit membership for a planned Job, not a minimum number of children. Use an optional, versioned planned-origin snapshot on that collection, linked back to the exact ProductionRun/scene and one completed generation/QA output. Keep the completed Job payload, its idempotency identity and ProviderCall history unchanged. Legacy series keep their existing membership rules. This avoids a second TalkingRun type, fake generation Jobs or retroactive series metadata on completed execution.

The origin pins project/run/scene and plan identity, Job and QA identities, output and Master hashes/interval, source/receipt/consent/license provenance, and the exact approved child-review evidence. Missing, mixed, orphaned or changed membership fails closed. Any planned Job requires this origin regardless of which API is used; removing the origin marker must not opt it into legacy admission. One shared application validation path must cover preparation, existing series review/admission entry points, replay and downstream planned-Run consumption. An existing Run ID alone is not a current-admission check.

Prepare an ordinary review-only audiovisual candidate with authoritative Master audio before asking for whole-result review. Bind preparation inputs, measured timing/streams and the exact candidate hash to durable evidence. Current technical QA checks duration and upstream copy evidence; it does not prove the provider output's audio equals the Master, so child approval cannot certify a later remux by inference. The continuity decision for this planned collection must bind the prepared candidate and origin snapshot. Admit the same reviewed bytes as the Run Asset/Clip; transformations or changed bytes require a new candidate and corresponding evidence. Keep this candidate ineligible for normal routing until admission.

Under the original policy, child U-Talking and independent whole-result continuity approval remain required, including for one child. No human decision is manufactured from technical QA or collection size. Reuse exact existing approvals when their subject and scope remain unchanged. D026 defines a separate versioned migration; until implemented, this bridge preserves both existing gates.

Preparation/admission rechecks current plan, media and applicable source receipt, rights/consent and license. Separate these checks from permission to start new inference: completed generation must not reserve or charge another provider call because its original call exhausted the budget. Local preparation still uses a durable Job with its own explicit cost/unknown-time evidence, cancellation and recovery. Implement preparation and reviewed admission as two bounded seams; their completion does not establish normal UI, real-media quality or render continuation.

## D026 — Consolidate Talking judgment on the exact output through a versioned policy

This decision authorizes a migration design, not a current runtime waiver. Policy v1 retains D025 and legacy child-plus-continuity semantics. Policy v2 initially applies only to newly created planned ProductionRuns using the existing single-child exact-preview path. Eligibility comes from current application/Worker checks for verified scoped capability, configuration, source admission, Master approval, rights and license; a caller's production/calibration label, a technical QA pass or a capability flag alone is insufficient. Unverified/new configurations remain blocked from ordinary dispatch and use separately bounded calibration work. An already admitted configuration does not need another calibration campaign merely because the topic changes. Legacy/direct evaluation and multi-child series remain v1 until their exact-output contracts are separately migrated.

V2 preserves every required child technical check, preview technical QA and the exact Master U-Voice gate. Its immutable whole-preview human decision explicitly covers visible sync, identity/likeness, mouth/face artifacts, source-performance retention, continuity and publishability. Every dimension must pass to approve; fail or unknown cannot approve. An incomplete judgment may remain awaiting review; only an explicit human rejection creates an immutable negative decision. This decision binds policy, ordered child/QA provenance, Master interval, preview bytes and technical report; admission consumes those same bytes. Child subjective review may remain unsubmitted in this lane and is never relabelled approved. A v1 continuity approval lacks this explicit scope and cannot be reinterpreted as v2 approval. Final U-Product remains separate.

Keep technical/authority failures, review concerns and negative judgments distinct. Failed/missing technical evidence or revoked authority blocks admission regardless of human opinion. A located low-confidence concern retains its source evidence, dimension and exact subject/interval; a human may answer it explicitly while reviewing the full preview, without a mandatory second visit. Missing visual detectors remain unassessed and are covered by the full human judgment, not invented confidence scores or blanket extra child reviews. Existing child/Run rejections are hard vetoes for that subject; another policy, collection ID or aggregate approval cannot erase them. Record structured rejection findings, with an interval when known and whole-result scope otherwise, for later bounded repair. A concern may be resolved; an immutable rejection may not be overwritten.

Persist a supported policy version when a new ProductionRun is created and propagate it through origin, preview evidence, review and admitted Run. Omission on old records/old clients means v1; unknown or inconsistent versions fail closed. V2 selection is available only at creation and never waives the eligibility checks above. No bulk rewrite of completed Jobs, approvals or in-flight runs. Preserve v1 canonical serialization and hashes when adding schema fields. A version-stripped v2 origin must not fall back to legacy; the owning production record and planned Job provenance are independently checked. Existing valid v1 approvals remain usable within their original scope.

Media or dependency replacement makes the affected aggregate evidence stale, not deleted. Changed child bytes/identity/order, QA, Master interval/bytes, source, plan or execution provenance requires a new exact candidate and applicable review; unrelated approved Masters, Runs and unchanged child evidence are retained. A new negative decision or unresolved blocking concern is rechecked on admission and consumption, even after preview preparation or approval. A redundant positive child review alone must not force re-encoding or repeat a valid full-preview judgment under v2. Repair creates a new immutable candidate/collection with predecessor provenance; unchanged rejected bytes cannot be laundered through a new ID. The actual repair action is a separate package.

Use one version-aware application rule for readiness, API submission, Worker preparation, admission/replay and Router/Assembly/Renderer consumption. Generated children and review-only previews remain unroutable. The review policy changes human-work scope, not source trust, commercial/provider admission, inference budgets or automatic approval. Missing evidence and unsupported paths return named stop states. Implement backend compatibility and normal UI as separate bounded seams; claim reduced manual work only after the corresponding workflow is exercised.

## D027 — Repair the supported dependency and preserve the review boundary

Repair eligibility requires a persisted failure bound to current media, plan and execution provenance. A localized observation does not create a localized editing capability: show the smallest independently executable and re-verifiable dependency and its full cost/review scope before confirmation. For the current single-take ProductionRun Voice lane, that unit is the entire take. Sentence-take recommendations, approximate ASR boundaries and a generic leading-silence warning do not authorize arbitrary splicing or trimming. Unknown/invalid failures stop for diagnosis; subjective delivery, reference or editorial changes require replanning. No new inference is justified merely by a rejected quality judgment without a supported corrective action.

The first Voice replacement seam is limited to a failed, unapproved, current single-take dependency before any downstream Talking/render work. A known transient generation failure or supported persisted copy-QA failure may propose one same-payload replacement with explicit whole-take scope; changed intent/configuration, incomplete QA or an approved/consumed Master cannot use that action. Preserve the original Job, audio, QA and review; the new output needs independent full-copy QA and exact U-Voice. Changing an approved Master later requires a separately versioned dependency replan. Pure presentation changes may preserve eligible, unchanged Master/Talking approvals, but must create a new render and final review; changed reviewed Talking-preview bytes/source/interval require the corresponding new candidate and review, never inheritance through a new ID.

Presentation recovery first uses eligible source selection and supported EditPlan treatments. A deterministic subtitle/text/canvas correction does not authorize new Voice/Talking inference, subtitle removal, unverified cropping, or an unnoticed editorial downgrade. Unsupported transformations or absent qualifying alternatives return a named replan/stop with required evidence. All repair writes consume the original production lineage's shared allowance atomically, retain predecessor and failure evidence, and declare bounded operations/charges before work. A linked successor cannot reset that allowance; independent new production needs its own explicit plan authorization and must retain asset-level negative evidence. These are implementation boundaries, not a claim that Voice/presentation repair or automatic quality detection is available today. Provider-specific current state remains in the Voice/Talking module spec.

## D028 — Retained render rejection constrains an adopted use, not the whole source

Preserve final human rejection against the exact render hash, retained plan/spec and original finding/reference/time/scope. Scene intervals reconstructed from the spec describe usage, not independent failure of every scene/frame or other intervals. A subtitle-conflict finding does not establish semantic mismatch or a removable new-caption layer. A reusable **source/presentation avoidance constraint** has separate authority: the creator explicitly adopts which future uses to avoid, classified as a configuration/use limitation. The candidate may list individual uses from the rejected composition, but their adoption is a planning choice, not retroactive interval-level human QA. General style preferences and measured crop defects remain separate.

Initially match only the same creator, exact source bytes/hash and exact interval plus effective output dimensions, visual role, framing/crop, panel mode, source-text handling, overlaid text/captions and relevant layout/style. Preserve original documents and name any legacy interpretation; missing identity or unreconstructable treatment is unresolved. A new Clip ID cannot evade the same use. Do not expand to overlapping/sub-intervals, parent sources, new generated descendants or all horizontal material. Changed uses remain subject to current suitability/admission; they are not automatically repaired or approved. A panel changes layout without removing source-text concerns.

Legacy assisted outputs may enter through normal explicit negative-evidence intake without fabricated Jobs, ProductionRuns, scene observations, publication or positive QA. Validate creator/project ownership, documents and current file hashes under configured data roots; retain original and adoption provenance separately. A reference/manifest alone is not trusted adoption authority. Reuse sufficient prior human judgments. Keep versioned candidate/adopt/disable history, creator/IP scope, evidence checks and dependency fingerprints. Disable removes future avoidance without deleting the rejection, approving the output or reviving older rules; changed evidence holds the rule, and changed IP requires explicit re-adoption.

Apply an adopted match to the source-plus-presentation recommendation before production, preserving asset admission and unaffected Master/Run approvals within their original scope. Select an independently eligible intent-preserving alternative or return a named unmet/replan/capture requirement; required Talking cannot silently become typography. Pinned selections and stale pending plans receive the same rule/current-version checks. No inference, transform, subtitle clearance or publication is authorized by avoidance. Require a demonstrated choice change beyond an additional stop message. If project/copy/admission already excludes the legacy Run, report no incremental effect and seek an applicable retained case without relaxing gates. This is one bounded application seam; full feedback/R1 completion still needs real normal-flow evidence.

## D029 — Admit execution for an evidenced purpose and preserve that scope downstream

R1 workflow quality and commercial provider admission are separate gates. The same normal planning/ProductionRun/Worker/QA/review flow may support an explicitly selected `internal_evaluation` purpose once scoped admission and output restrictions are implemented end to end. Default/omitted purpose preserves the existing `commercial_production` dispatch checks; unknown values fail closed. Existing Jobs, hashes, source receipts and human judgments are not rewritten. This decision defines a migration, not current execution permission.

An internal label or a non-commercial provider flag does not establish permission for the intended activity, including product development. Require a trusted, project-scoped ProviderUseAdmission bound to the exact capability/configuration and model-artifact identity, inspectable license evidence/version/hash, the intended evaluation use and permitted local generation/derivation/review/export scope. A separately configured admission operator adopts verified evidence; caller-supplied actor strings, general API access, source-review credentials, model output or a nonempty license reference alone cannot issue authority. Unknown, incompatible or missing evidence stops. No current provider is admitted by this decision; commercial status remains unchanged.

Keep capability, permitted use and per-run execution authorization distinct. Existing real QA/U-Voice/U-Talking evidence may support explicit configuration-scoped capability adoption where provenance is sufficient; an exact asset approval does not verify all durations/settings, authorize new inference or grant a license. Missing configuration evidence requires a bounded gap-specific check. An admitted unchanged configuration is reusable for later topics within its project/use scope without repeating calibration or already answered human review. All existing source suitability, consent, quality, budget, review-policy and repair limits remain.

Freeze purpose and admission references/version on new Runs and include them in preflight, dispatch/idempotency, Job and Worker execution identity. A shared application resolver checks current receipt, evidence bytes, purpose, project and actual provider/model/artifact/runtime/machine/configuration before reservation and at subsequent resume/repair/admission/consumption boundaries. Revocation, changed terms/evidence/configuration or stripped/inconsistent origin blocks new use without deleting historical evidence. Repair/successors inherit purpose and the original allowance. Local-only evaluation is the initial scope; remote or public use requires separately admitted rights and execution scope. Do not use the legacy evaluation environment switch as the normal lane.

Carry purpose and origin restrictions through generated AudioAsset/Master, Talking children/preview/Run, derived media and Render. A descendant cannot become commercial-safe because it was copied, assigned a new ID, reimported or placed in a new project; known hashes/origins must be checked, and ambiguous lineage cannot confer commercial clearance. Mixed dependencies retain all applicable restrictions. A later commercial route requires separate whole-lineage rights evidence; it cannot mutate the original evaluation approval. User review of quality does not approve a change of use. Evaluation output may be previewed and downloaded locally for the admitted review purpose with visible scope and a retained manifest; it is not publication-ready authority. Existing factual publication records stay factual, not proof of license, and must not conceal linked restrictions; recording an external event is distinct from authorizing it. Normal commercial use/publication must reject restricted or unresolved lineage. Local file download cannot prevent out-of-product copying, so no DRM or universal prevention claim is made.

The implementation seam begins with an inert, versioned admission record and deterministic resolver/truth-table tests. Evaluation dispatch remains disabled until Run/Worker and downstream scope enforcement are complete for the supported lane. Follow with normal UI and evidence adoption, then separately bounded runtime verification. R1's two normal new-topic trials still require fresh creator Voice/Talking, authorized real-media use and final quality review; typography-only output cannot substitute. No provider shopping, new graph engine or repeated paid replanning is a prerequisite to closing this policy seam. For the first controlled OmniVoice lane, select a separately prepared CPU-only package candidate; the historical CUDA wheel is unsupported under the CPU recipe because import-time CUDA libraries and external search branches precede device selection. Candidate selection grants no execution or installation authority and does not certify a CPU wheel's own native closure. Preserve the original installation and existing OS trust boundary. New recipe versions must explicitly identify component precision and any finite source-bound computational-buffer rule; loaded values cannot authorize their own exceptions. Own the main/auxiliary loading sequence and bounded decoded-reference input through documented/source-verified entry points, without silently casting checkpoint weights; third-party sources remain unmodified except for the staged SoundFile loader and the separate PCM-only audio-helper derivations explicitly bounded below. Keep old recipe meanings, hashes and receipts unchanged; adopting a new effective runtime/precision recipe requires new evidence identity. Implementation contracts and actual compatibility evidence remain separate. For the exact reviewed CPU pair, three byte-exact resource2 forms (two asInvoker/uiAccess=false and one empty assembly, enumerated in ROADMAP) may enter a separately inventoried private-root-context recipe. Recognition binds raw manifest/resource identity/codepage and whole member provenance; it is neither data-only classification nor permission to load. Require an independently created root-only context prediction before target loading, exact source/parent and no configuration/dependent roster/file/COM redirection, then matching actual module association and unchanged effective/prior contexts. Preserve existing opaque root-flag/current-user-language rules and old Common Controls behavior; unknown shape/query failure remains unsupported.
